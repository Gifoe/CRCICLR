# B-HiCER data protocol

Only `method_development` subjects are accessible in Stage-0. In each five-fold rotation, fold r is evaluation, fold r+1 is one-sided calibration, and the remaining three folds are meta training. Formal calibration, internal final, and CAP remain closed unless all Stage-0 gates pass. Context labels are exposed only through the budgeted query oracle; Future labels are exposed only after a hashed risk decision.
