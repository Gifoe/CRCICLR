# Claim audit

The permitted claim is conditional: identity reduction alone does not prove
task-useful persistent information was removed. A positive selective-loss
result requires valid replica competence, Protected assignment, positive
replica-normalized SPL, and the frozen subject-level confidence rule. Rescue
results are causal only for STATUS_D; STATUS_C is a functional restoration
diagnostic. V1.1 is exploratory and cannot be described as preregistered.
