# Claim audit

The strong claim is allowed only when the frozen full-representation identity
competence check passes, DDA-B/C reproduce, MD beats M0, and MD beats MI under
the held-run comparison. Otherwise the claim is limited or unsupported as
reported in `EXP3_FINAL_REPORT.json`.

This experiment never claims that Protected persistence contains zero identity
information, that identity and utility are independent, or that a
nonsignificant identity increment proves zero contribution. DDA-A's historical
behavioral-null failure is not silently repaired or used as a gate for this
decision-grounding closure.
