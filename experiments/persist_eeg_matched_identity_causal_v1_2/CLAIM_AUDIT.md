# Claim audit

The only permitted positive claim is the predeclared Theory-3 statement on the
reused development resource, and only if continuous G2 and the full G3 gate
pass. If continuous identity is not measurable or does not transfer to held
out subjects, the theory is `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`; if G2 passes but Delta_H fails,
the theory is `NOT_SUPPORTED`. Top-1 or secondary metrics cannot override the
primary metric.
