# P/C mechanism closure (seed 0)

Independent frozen mechanism audit for EEGNet and EEGConformer on OpenBMI MI/SSVEP, folds 0–4. This experiment is gated on the reviewed protected-complement coupling audit. It does not use final-heldout data, alter the frozen upstream P/C definitions, or design a performance model.

The first gate checks the exact upstream hashes, all 20 original cells, classifier-input and layer P+C reconstruction, retained random controls, and the separately provenanced prediction correction. The earlier label-conditioned future-error AUROC is invalid; its exploratory correction does not establish criterion C. The unchanged criterion-A coupling result is the upstream mechanism evidence.

Training-checkpoint availability is audited separately before any trajectory work. The initial availability inventory and its v2 endpoint check are preserved as superseded evidence: they did not account correctly for `latest.pt` and EEGConformer early stopping. The source-specific v3 audit verifies historical selected/final checkpoint endpoints for all 20 cells. It verifies no 10/25/50/75% intermediate snapshots. Those stages therefore require a faithful seed-0 baseline replay; a nonidentical replay must be labeled `RETRAINED_REPLICA_TRAJECTORY` and cannot be presented as the historical training path.

The first adjacent-mediation engineering probe failed because its hybrid activation was flattened before entering a convolutional successor stage. Its `FAIL_CLOSED` record and server log are retained. The corrected v2 probe passed on EEGNet/OpenBMI MI/fold0 using two TRAIN trials: all four adjacent transitions exactly matched the frozen native forward (`max_abs=0`), with finite mediation quantities and no final-heldout access. This is an implementation check, not a 20-cell mechanism result. Its checked source hashes and outputs are in `probe/` and on the original server.

A subsequent EEGConformer/OpenBMI MI/fold0 adjacent probe passed all six transitions with exact native-forward agreement. The v4 engineering probes then passed the prespecified early/middle/late-to-final mediation paths for both architectures (three paths each), alongside the adjacent checks. These two-TRAIN-trial probes establish execution and shape integrity only; they do not establish effect size, random specificity, subject inference, or cross-task generality.

An additional TRAIN-only audit found that the prompt's orthogonal `Q_final Q_final.T` does **not** reproduce the frozen canonical final Protected: mean relative P discrepancy was 0.805 for EEGNet and 1.005 for EEGConformer on OpenBMI MI/fold0. The user delegated resolution. `protocol/FINAL_PROJECTOR_AMENDMENT.md` therefore preserves the original canonical (oblique) final P and keeps orthogonal pathway projections at intermediate stages, with an explicit geometric boundary. The amended v5 probes passed in both architectures; exact final P agreed with the frozen canonical decomposition within `3.6e-7`, all adjacent/long-range checks remained finite, and native-forward maximum difference remained zero. The two protocol documents and hashes are frozen in `protocol/PROTOCOL_LOCK.sha256` before any full cell or outer-development mechanism evaluation.
