# Related-method boundary

PERSIST-RE does not claim random-effects neural networks, domain episodes, or
GroupDRO as individually novel.  Neural mixed-effects models motivate the
low-rank parameterization; ARMED-style methods and DANN motivate the explicit
adversarial control; MLDG/domain meta-learning motivate held-subject episodes;
GroupDRO is a generic subject-robust control; training-time domain experts are
another nearby family.  The bounded claim is the evidence-driven combination
of training-only decision-level random effects with direct population-only
optimization on pseudo-unseen subjects, without representation invariance or
transport.
