# Hypothesis ledger

## H0 — preregistered before outcome evaluation

Real invariance objectives may reduce subject identity without necessarily
removing intervention-defined Protected persistence. Therefore I1, I2, and I3
are tested separately and no rescue claim is attempted unless all three pass.

## H1 — selective rescue

For an eligible family, a same-rank Protected residual should outperform both
the invariant representation and a high-persistence residual selected without
signed task utility. Random and PCA residuals are secondary capacity controls.

## Frozen alternatives

The roster is limited to EEGNet+GRL, EEG-DG, and SCLDGN. V6--V8 adaptation,
routers, action banks, Conformer blending, and outcome-driven method additions
are excluded. Negative outcomes terminate the experiment rather than trigger
unbounded search.
