"
        "Treat each source subject as an environment P_s(X,Y). ERM commits to the uniform empirical subject distribution; one DRO model commits to one selected worst-case distribution. Since prior evidence does not identify one universally correct representation intervention, subject task gradients provide a local risk-disagreement signature. Leading gradient-covariance modes form a low-rank approximation to population-risk uncertainty. Competent predictors are marginalized rather than selected at inference, and the projection keeps a first-order descent component for uniform risk. This is an approximation/modeling assumption, not a claim that a future subject is literally a convex combination of source subjects.
