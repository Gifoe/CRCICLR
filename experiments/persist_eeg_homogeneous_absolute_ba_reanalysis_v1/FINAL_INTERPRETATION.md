# Final interpretation

## Direct answers

1. EL is higher than EE by point estimate in 4/4 blocks. Equal-block EL minus EE is +1.056 pp (95% CI [+0.530, +1.599]).
2. EL is higher than LL by point estimate in 2/4 blocks. Equal-block EL minus LL is -0.011 pp (95% CI [-0.331, +0.309]).
3. EL exceeds EE in 4/4 blocks and LL in 2/4 blocks under identical subject-equal aggregation.
4. Global equal-block EL minus EE is +1.056 pp (95% CI [+0.530, +1.599]).
5. Global equal-block EL minus LL is -0.011 pp (95% CI [-0.331, +0.309]).
6. Global equal-block EL minus HOMOAVG is +0.522 pp (95% CI [+0.368, +0.689]).

## Complementarity versus utility

EL is the highest descriptive exclusive-correct family in every block: True. Equal-block exclusive-correct percentages are EE=10.320, LL=11.081, EL=13.173.
EL is the highest absolute-BA family in 2/4 blocks. Thus descriptive complementarity does not consistently translate into the highest absolute BA against both homogeneous controls.

## Global weighting and fixed-rule robustness

- Dataset-balanced EL minus EE: +0.801 pp (95% CI [+0.217, +1.367]).
- Dataset-balanced EL minus LL: +0.036 pp (95% CI [-0.338, +0.443]).
- Dataset-balanced EL minus HOMOAVG: +0.418 pp (95% CI [+0.278, +0.562]).
- PROB50 does not materially change the global conclusion: no contrast sign change and no shift over 0.5 pp. See PROB50_ROBUSTNESS.csv.

## Claim boundary

Heterogeneous-specific superiority is not supported unless EL is convincingly superior to both EE and LL in this ordinary matched absolute-BA comparison. The EL minus LL result, rather than a post-hoc best-constituent oracle quantity, is decisive for that boundary.
