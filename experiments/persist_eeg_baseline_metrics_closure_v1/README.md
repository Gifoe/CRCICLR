# Baseline metrics closure v1

This branch contains the original read-only audit and its preserved STOP gate, followed by an approved protocol amendment (`protocol/TRUE_OUTER_AMENDMENT_20260916.md`). Under that amendment, WBCIC uses the final true-outer 10 subjects; the original numerical targets from the disjoint V8 internal cohort are not used as true-outer references. `protocol/REGRESSION_GATE.json` describes the original pre-amendment STOP, not the terminal status of the amended analysis.

Frozen inference, unified metrics/MAC profiling, and the ModernTCN/Medformer PSWA extension are complete without new training or checkpoint selection. Five models have all 4 tasks × 15 checkpoints. LiteBN has 15/15 for OpenBMI MI/ERP, 13/15 for OpenBMI SSVEP, and 5/15 for WBCIC MI; the latter two cells are explicitly incomplete and were not retrained. See `outputs/CLOSURE_VALIDATION.json` and `outputs/BASELINE_METRICS_CLOSURE_REPORT.md`. Original experiments remain untouched; runtime tensors, raw data and checkpoints are excluded from Git.
