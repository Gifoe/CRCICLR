The pre-outcome lock was committed before any development outcome index or label was materialized. Training and SSPG certificates used only source/refit subjects. Outcome subjects and B_out were opened only in aggregate after all ten contexts completed. WBCIC outer-10 and OpenBMI sealed/confirmation data were not opened; seed 1 and seed 2 were not run.

Lock safety flags: `outcome_accessed_before_lock=false`, `WBCIC_outer_opened=false`, `OpenBMI_sealed_opened=false`.
