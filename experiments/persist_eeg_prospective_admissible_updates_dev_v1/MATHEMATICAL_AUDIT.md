# Mathematical audit

The exact two-halfspace Euclidean projection is solved by active-set enumeration (none, A-only, B-only, both). The runner checks C1 future admissibility, C2 joint admissibility, the nearest feasible toy solution, C3's fixed halfway correction, BN freezing and the seed-0/schedule contracts. Machine-readable checks are in `results/MATH_TOY_TEST.json`.
