# Scientific hypothesis

The prospective PUD-to-action route is closed by the preceding headroom and
mechanism audit.  This experiment tests a different, falsifiable claim:

> A source-certified persistent, task-useful, finite-decision-consequential
> function can be distilled into an independent EEGNet pathway and protected
> from target-history adaptation.  If this structural boundary is useful, it
> must outperform a strong legally reconstructed EEGNet, a capacity-matched
> dual-path control, identity/random protected controls, and PUD with both
> pathways adapted.

The primary setting is OpenBMI MI Session 1 history to Session 2 future on the
40-subject V8_SEARCH development scope.  The 14-subject OpenBMI internal
holdout is excluded before any EEG row is materialized.  WBCIC is reached only
if the frozen OpenBMI G1/G6/G9 gates pass.

Failure is informative.  The terminal failure state is
`PERSIST_NET_CONSTRUCTIVE_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_SUPPORTED`; it cannot be relabeled by
lowering a gate or inventing another architecture after outcome inspection.
