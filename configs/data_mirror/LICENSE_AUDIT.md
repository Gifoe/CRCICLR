# PERSIST-EEG raw mirror license audit

Audit date: 2026-08-10

## OpenBMI / Lee2019 — NEMAR distributions

Dataset: OpenBMI Motor Imagery / ERP-P300 / SSVEP  
Official source: NEMAR (`nm000338`, `nm000323`, `nm000273`)  
Version: v1.0.1  
License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)  
Redistribution permitted: Yes, subject to preserving the license and notices and meeting GPL-3.0 conveyance requirements.  
Attribution requirements: Retain upstream copyright/license notices and cite Lee et al., the original GigaDB DOI `10.5524/100542`, the paper DOI `10.1093/gigascience/giz002`, and the applicable NEMAR dataset DOI.  
Raw private mirror permitted: Yes. The selected source is the published NEMAR BIDS distribution; it is mirrored byte-for-byte and remains under GPL-3.0. It is not represented as the original GigaDB archive.  
Evidence URLs: https://nemar.org/dataset/nm000338 ; https://nemar.org/dataset/nm000323 ; https://nemar.org/dataset/nm000273 ; https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html  
Decision: PASS for the NEMAR-published files, provided their license/provenance files remain included and the mirror stays unmodified.

Official-source fallback: GigaDB dataset `100542`. GigaDB declares its dataset content CC0; the fallback remains fully attributed despite CC0 not requiring attribution. Evidence: https://gigadb.org/dataset/100542 and https://gigadb.org/site/termsofuse . Decision: PASS for byte-preserving fallback files.
