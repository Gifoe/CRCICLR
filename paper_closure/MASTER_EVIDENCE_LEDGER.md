# PERSIST-EEG master evidence ledger

This is the canonical human-readable companion to `MASTER_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`. It contains 56 evidence rows in chronological order (`-3..0`, `1..52`) and uses repository-relative paths only. The row-level CSV is authoritative for the exact 33-field schema; the tables below retain the audited scientific context and supersession history.

## Evidence-source priority

1. A passing final validator or integrity record tied to immutable compact outputs.
2. The scientifically repaired final report tied to its frozen protocol and exact result files.
3. Frozen machine-readable JSON/CSV summaries when no standalone validator exists.
4. Historical final reports and protocol locks, explicitly downgraded when later work repairs or supersedes them.
5. Git inventory, branch labels, commit chronology, filenames, and old chat summaries only for provenance, never to invent a scientific value.

The newest timestamp is not automatically authoritative. A repair supersedes only the claim or measurement it actually repairs. Missing values remain marked `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_REPORTED`; they are not inferred from directory names, row counts, or nearby experiments.

## Statistical-unit rules

- The biological subject is the primary scientific unit whenever a subject-level estimand or inference is reported.
- Seeds, folds, runs, directions, intervention cells, trials, centroids, and paired backbone rows are repeated algorithmic or measurement units, not additional people.
- Shared biological cohorts across tasks/backbones/settings are not independent replications. Paired comparisons remain paired, and paired backbone rows stay clustered within subject.
- A report-level run bootstrap is retained as the exact historical estimand when that was frozen, but it is not relabeled as biological-subject inference.
- Trial-level and subject-run counts are never reported as biological sample size.

## Conflict and supersession rules

- Negative frozen primary terminals remain negative even when secondary regime, oracle, or subgroup signals look favorable.
- A generic ensemble gain is not PERSIST-specific evidence. In particular, the historical V2 policy gain is superseded by `ENSEMBLE_EXPLAINS_V2_GAIN`.
- Fold-incorrect cached OpenBMI baseline/dynamic results are invalid for quantitative use after the fold-correct repair.
- P4B remains `P4B_IDENTITY_RELIABILITY_CONDITION_NOT_SUPPORTED`; P4C contributes only a coarse partial safety boundary and does not convert P4B into support.
- P4D remains `P4D_METHOD_LEVEL_BRIDGE_NOT_SUPPORTED`; one manipulation-competent method cannot support method-general inference.
- SCST Repair-2 remains `TRANSPORT_VALIDITY_NOT_SUPPORTED`, and the constructive route closes as `FINAL_CONSTRUCTIVE_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- SCAA Stage-0 and Reliability Stage-0.5 retain partial scientific terminals but remain explicitly unauthorized for development.
- No outer or sealed-outcome access was performed to compile or validate this ledger.
